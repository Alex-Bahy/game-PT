How To Play

2-Team Turn Based Rules

Teams take turns answering questions. Each turn has a default timer (15, 20, or 30 seconds).

Scoring & Steal Mechanism

  • 100 points for a correct answer on your team's direct turn.
  • Steal Opportunity: If a team answers incorrectly, the opposing team gets 1 steal chance with 10 seconds on the timer for 50 points!
  • 0 points for wrong answers or running out of time.

5 Gameplay Rounds

Questions cover Picture Guessing, Order Sequencing, Reading Comprehension, Real-World Scenarios, and Visual Diagrams based on Grade XI SMK Procedure Text topics.
